For starters, I am so glad you want to learn more about me! I started out doing suport tickets through live customer interactions and just a few years (and a couple hundred support tickets later), I'm the one solo-securing an entire learning institution, investigating potential phishing reports, and writing the detailed information for tickets escalated to higher positions within my organization's IT network and security teams. The one thing that has not changed since day one has to be my relentless desire to learn and teach others.

Right now I'm doing working toward a SOC analyst seat. Aside from utilizing my skills and knowledge in cybersecurity frameworks and best practices, it's the most direct line from the log analysis and phishing triage I already do into a full-time security role. Nonetheless, I cant help but admit that what actually keeps me up at night is lower in the stack: reverse engineering, exploit development, pulling hardware apart just to see how it thinks, and basically getting as close to the 1s and 0s that run our entire world. Most of that shows up in the homelab and hardware projects elsewhere on this site more than it does on paper.

With that in mind, I hope you enjoy your time learning about me, the things I do, and subjects I will never learn enough about.
